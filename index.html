Why Most Church & Charity Websites Fail (And How We Fix It)

Every week your site stays slow, confusing or hard to give through, visitors leave and gifts go unsent. You never see them go. That is exactly what makes it expensive.

The Slow-Data Killer

⛪ ChurchesA first-time visitor on a weak Safaricom or Airtel connection waits for your service times, gives up, and visits a church that loaded.

🌍 NGOsA grant-maker or field partner opens your site on a slow link, watches it crawl, and moves on to the next applicant.

Our fix: we strip out the heavy junk and optimize every page and image, so your site opens instantly even on weak 3G.

Friction-Heavy Giving

⛪ ChurchesMembers must copy a Paybill and account number, switch apps, and hope they typed it right. Many never finish.

🌍 NGOsDiaspora and international donors find no simple card option, or a form that only takes local money. The gift never arrives.

Our fix: M-Pesa and card giving built into the page, so a gift takes seconds, from Kibera to Kansas.

The 5-Second Confusion

⛪ ChurchesNew families can’t quickly find your service times, location or how to connect, so they never walk through your door.

🌍 NGOsInstitutional donors can’t see where the money goes or what you have achieved, and trust drains before they read your proposal.

Our fix: layouts that put service times and live impact numbers in front of people within seconds.

This is not a design problem. It is a trust and revenue problem, and it is fixable.

How much does a plan cost?

Every project receives a flat, transparent setup fee paired with a managed monthly or annual support retainer. You will get a customized fixed-price plan within 24 hours of your free digital audit.

Do your websites follow Kenya’s data protection law?

We build every platform in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act: forms collect only what is needed, a clear privacy notice is shown, and card details are never stored on your site. Your organization stays responsible for its own ODPC registration, and we can point you in the right direction.

Which payment methods are supported?

Our platforms handle local mobile money via M-Pesa STK push and Airtel Money for your local supporters, alongside secure global card processing (Visa/Mastercard/PayPal) for international donors or diaspora giving.

Can our internal team update the site easily?

Yes. We set up an incredibly simple, low-code dashboard for updating notices, media files, or fundraising milestones, and we provide full training to your staff upon handover.

Do we own the website?

Yes. You own the site, your data and your domain. We never lock you in.

What if you don’t deliver what you promised?

You get your money back. If within 90 days of going live your site isn’t delivering what we agreed in writing, we refund 100% of what you paid, and you keep the work and the domain.
