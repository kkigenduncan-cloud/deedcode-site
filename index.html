Does this sound like you?

If one of these made you nod, please know it isn’t your fault.

Most software is made for everyone, which is exactly why it so often ends up fitting no one. These are the things people tell us in their very first message.

“Our donation form loses people halfway through, and we can’t work out why.”

“Our volunteer schedule lives in a shared spreadsheet that breaks every other week.”

“Our membership system has to talk to three other tools, and it simply doesn’t.”

“I have an idea for a tool that doesn’t exist yet, anywhere.”

And if you’ve been living with a workaround for a year, that’s exactly the kind of problem we love being handed, because solving it gives you your time and peace of mind back.

Why this matters

Technology is quiet, until the day it fails.

A broken donation page doesn’t only lose a gift, it loses a donor’s confidence, and a clumsy volunteer system can cost you the very people who wanted to help. On that day, technology is the only thing anyone notices, so we build with one goal in mind: that day never comes.

Questions

The things people ask us first.

How much will it cost?

You’ll know the full price before we start anything, because we think you deserve to decide with all the facts in front of you, not discover the number halfway through.

What if I don’t know what I need?

That’s completely normal, and it’s actually how most people begin. Just describe what’s going wrong in your own words, and we’ll work out the right fix together, since figuring that out is the part you’re really paying us for.

What if something already solves my problem?

Then we’ll tell you, even if it means you don’t need to hire us. If a tool we have, or one that already exists, fits your situation, pointing you to it is simply the honest thing to do.

What happens after my tool goes live?

We stay. If something breaks or you need a change, you’ll reach the person who built it, not a ticket number, because software only helps you if it keeps working next month and next year.

How quickly will I hear back?

Within one business day, and often sooner. Every message is read by a real person who writes back properly, with no copy-and-paste answers.

Do you only work with churches and NGOs?

They’re where we began, and they will always be close to our hearts, but today we build for anyone doing meaningful, honest work, whether it’s non-profit or not.

Whatever brought you here, the easiest next step is simply to tell us about it.

It might be a problem you’re tired of working around, a tool you wish existed, or an idea that won’t leave you alone. Send us a few honest words, and within one business day a real person will write back with a clear plan and the full price, so you can decide with nothing hidden and nothing to lose.
