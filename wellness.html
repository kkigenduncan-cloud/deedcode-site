Ask on WhatsApp
Can members book classes on WhatsApp?

Yes. They pick a class or appointment slot in chat, and it fills automatically up to the limit you set.

Can we offer packages and promotions?

Yes. Packages, renewals and special offers can each have their own message and price.

What about members who freeze their membership?

You can pause a membership and the system stops the reminders until the new date.

Do I need a Paybill or Till number first?

You’ll need one to receive money, and we work with both. If you don’t have one yet, or you don’t have Safaricom Daraja API access, we help you set it up.
