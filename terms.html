1. About these terms

These terms cover your use of deedcode.com and the general basis on which DeedCode (“we”, “us”) provides services. Each project is also covered by a written agreement or fixed-price plan we send you. If that agreement and these terms differ, the written agreement wins.

2. Our services

We design, build and support websites and digital tools for churches, ministries and NGOs, including giving pages, visitor and donor forms, and content dashboards. What exactly you get, and when, is listed in your written plan.

3. The free digital audit

The audit is free and comes with no obligation. We review the site or presence you tell us about and send our findings and a fixed-price plan, usually within 24 hours of getting your details. The timing is our target, not a legal deadline, and may vary with the details you send.

4. Prices and payments

Every plan has a fixed setup fee and, where you choose it, a monthly or annual support retainer. We state the price in writing before any work starts, and we will not add charges you have not agreed to. Payment milestones and methods (for example M-Pesa or bank transfer) are set out in your agreement. Costs paid directly to other companies, such as domain names, hosting, SMS credits or payment-provider fees, are separate unless your plan says otherwise.

5. What we need from you

  • Accurate information, and timely feedback and approvals.
  • That you own, or have permission to use, the text, photos, logos, audio and video you give us.
  • That you follow the laws that apply to your own organisation, including registering with the ODPC and handling your supporters’ data lawfully where required.
  • That you keep your logins safe and tell us quickly if you think they have been compromised.

6. Third-party services

We keep ownership of our own tools, templates and code that existed before your project, and we give you the right to use them as part of your site. Unless you ask us not to, we may show the finished work in our portfolio.
