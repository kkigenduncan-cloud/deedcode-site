1. Who these terms are between

These terms are between DeedCode, Nairobi, Kenya ("we") and the business that buys our services ("you"). Your written plan or quote forms part of these terms.

2. What we provide

We build, connect and run the WhatsApp, M-Pesa and website services described in your written plan. We aim to have your system built and tested within 7 days of receiving everything we need, as set out on our guarantee page.

3. Fees

You pay a one-time setup fee and a monthly care and hosting fee, as stated in your written plan. Care and hosting is month to month. Fees charged by third parties, such as Meta message fees and Safaricom transaction charges, are paid by you at cost and are listed in writing before you start.

4. Your money

Payments from your customers settle directly into your own Till or Paybill. We never hold or move your money.

5. What we need from you

Accurate business details, prices and branding, access to your WhatsApp and M-Pesa accounts, and prompt answers to our questions. You are responsible for what you tell your customers and for having their agreement before we message them for you.

6. Third parties

Meta, Safaricom and other providers make their own decisions about approvals, rules and fees. Delays or changes on their side are outside our control.

7. Results

We promise that what we agree in writing is built and runs as agreed. We do not promise any particular amount of extra revenue or time saved, because that depends on your business and customers.

8. Ownership

You own your website, your customer data, your domain and your WhatsApp number. We keep ownership of our own tools and methods.

9. Ending the agreement

You can stop care and hosting at any time, and you keep your website, data, domain and WhatsApp number. We can end the service if you stop paying or use it unlawfully.

10. Our responsibility

To the extent the law allows, our total responsibility to you for any claim is limited to the fees you paid us in the 12 months before the claim. Nothing here limits responsibility that cannot be limited by law.

11. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Kenya, and the courts of Kenya have jurisdiction.
