The people behind the problem-solving.

DeedCode started with a simple belief: doing good shouldn't mean settling for bad technology.

Why DeedCode exists.

Too many people doing meaningful work are stuck with websites that don't represent them, tools that don't quite fit, or no technology partner at all — because most agencies build for whoever can pay the most, not for what actually matters.

We started DeedCode to change that. Not as a side project or a favor we do occasionally, but as the whole point of the company. If your work is good, your technology should be too.

What we believe, in five words.

Purpose

We build for a reason, not just a fee.

Excellence

Good causes deserve great technology.

Integrity

We do what's right, even when no one's checking.

Service

Your mission comes before our convenience.

Impact

We measure success by the change made, not the code shipped.

Who's building this.

DeedCode is led by Duncan K. Kigen, founder and architect, based in Nairobi, Kenya. Every project that comes through DeedCode is held to the same standard — because the name on the company is also the name on the work.

Let's build something worth the trust.

If you're doing meaningful work and need technology that keeps up, we'd like to hear about it.
