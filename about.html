Our story

Behind every tool we build is one belief: good work deserves good technology.

DeedCode started with a simple idea, that doing good should never mean settling for bad technology, and everything we do since then has been an attempt to prove it.

Why DeedCode exists.

Too many people doing meaningful work are stuck with websites that don’t represent them, tools that almost fit, or no technology partner at all, because most agencies build for whoever can pay the most instead of for what matters.

So we started DeedCode to change that, not as a side project or an occasional favour, but as the whole point of the company.

Why it matters more than people think.

A broken donation page doesn’t just lose a gift, it loses a donor’s confidence, and a clumsy volunteer system wastes a volunteer’s willingness to come back. Technology is quiet infrastructure until the moment it fails, and we build so it never has to be noticed.

What we believe

Five words we measure every piece of work against.

They aren’t slogans, they’re the questions we ask ourselves before anything leaves our hands.

Purpose

We build for a reason, not only for a fee.

Excellence

Good causes deserve great technology.

Integrity

We do what’s right, even when nobody is checking.

Service

Your mission comes before our convenience.

Impact

We measure success by the change made, not by the code shipped.

DK

DeedCode is led by Duncan K. Kigen, founder and architect, based in Nairobi, Kenya, and the name on the company is also the name on the work.

Close enough to understand, serious enough to deliver.

We build from Kenya for the kind of work being done across Africa and beyond, and we work with organisations across the region and anywhere else the mission takes you.
