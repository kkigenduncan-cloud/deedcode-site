What “running as agreed” means: your written checklist

  • Your automations (guest and prayer follow-up, sermon summaries, giving and receipts, or your live impact tracker) are built, connected and running as described in your written plan
  • Giving by M-Pesa and card is built, live and tested before you pay the final milestone
  • Thank-you receipts and notifications go out automatically
  • Your site opens fast on weak mobile data
  • Your team has been trained and can run it with confidence
We guarantee delivery of what we agree in writing, not donor behaviour or the exact number of hours saved. Delays caused by you or by third parties, such as payment-provider or WhatsApp approvals, do not count against the 7 days. Read the full guarantee terms.
Questions

Questions Pastors And NGO Directors Ask Us

Straight answers, no jargon. Still unsure? Message us and a real person replies.

Ask on WhatsApp
What exactly is the care & automation plan?

It is a small monthly plan in which we keep your automations running, your site safe and fast, and your team supported on WhatsApp, so you never have to worry about the technical side.

Is the price really fixed?

Yes. Your price is written down before we start, and we do not add charges you have not agreed to.

Why is it priced so low?

We reuse proven engines instead of building from scratch every time, and we would rather help many churches and NGOs run well than a few big ones. Your price is fixed and written down before we start, so there are no surprise bills.

Are there any other costs?

Your payment provider charges its own normal transaction fees, and some tools, such as WhatsApp business messaging, may carry small usage charges. We list every third-party cost in writing before you start, so you always know the full picture.

How can it really be live in 7 days?

Because we do not start from a blank page. Our Church and NGO engines are already built and tested, so we set them up with your name, service times, branding and payment details instead of reinventing them. Once we have what we need from you, your system is built, connected and live within 7 days.

What if our organisation is bigger?

For denominations, multi-branch ministries and larger NGOs, we scope your workflows with you and quote a fixed price in writing. Start with the free time-leak audit and we will recommend the right fit.

What is the Founding Partner offer?

Our first 10 churches get the complete Church Automation Suite for a lower setup price, in return for an honest testimonial and permission to share your measured hours-saved results after 90 days. We never share your members’ details, and the offer closes once the places are taken.

Can we pay in instalments?

Yes. You pay half of the setup fee to start and the other half when you go live, and your monthly care plan is month to month.

Can we start small?

Yes. The Guest Welcome Starter (KES 4,900) and the Donor Checkout Starter (KES 9,900) are made for exactly that, and you can upgrade to the complete system any time.

What if the automation doesn’t run the way you promised?

Then you get every shilling back and you keep the site. For 90 days after you go live, we stand behind exactly what we agreed in writing. You are also protected by our 7-day go-live guarantee and our month-to-month, no lock-in care plan. You can read the full terms on our guarantee page.

Can we leave any time?

Yes. Your care plan is month to month, so you can stop whenever you like. You keep your site, your data and your domain, with nothing owed.

Do we own the website and our data?

Yes. You own your site, your data and your domain, and we never lock you in.

Is M-Pesa giving safe for our members?

Yes. Giving uses M-Pesa STK Push, so your member simply approves the gift with their own PIN on their own phone. Card payments are handled by licensed payment providers, and card details are never stored on your website.
