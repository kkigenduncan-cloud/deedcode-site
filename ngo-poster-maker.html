Questions

Things people ask before they start

Can I change the fonts and colours?

Yes, completely. Every text can use any free Google font, any colour by hex code, picker or palette, and you can add shadow, outline, spacing and rotation.

Is the font library free to use?

Yes, Google Fonts are free for personal and commercial use under open licences, so your designs are safe to print and sell.

Does it copy the reference I upload?

No, it learns from colours, mood and layout, and builds your own design, so you never copy someone else’s artwork. Please only use references you are free to use as inspiration.

How much is the clean version?

One simple price for any design, in your own currency, shown beside the download button.

Why is the preview small?

To protect your design before you unlock it. The clean file is full quality and ready for print or social media.
