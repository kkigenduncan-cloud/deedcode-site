Where your gift goes

Eight programs. One purpose: change a life in a way that lasts.

Community Meals & Food Packs

Hot meals, food parcels and feeding drives for children, families and the elderly facing hunger every day.

Infant & Maternal Support

Diapers, baby clothing and hygiene kits for new mothers working to give their babies a clean start.

Literacy & Learning

Books, learning materials and reading programs for children who deserve real access to education.

Mentorship & Life Skills

Mentors who believe in young people, guide their choices and build their confidence.

Women & Youth Empowerment

Skills training, business support and leadership development, so poverty becomes a season, not a sentence.

Medical Camps & Health Support

Free medical camps bringing screening, treatment and advice to families who cannot otherwise reach care.

Support for Persons With Disabilities

Mobility aids, accessibility support and dedicated care for persons with disabilities and their families.

Emergency Relief

Immediate food, shelter and emotional support when crisis hits: floods, illness or sudden family breakdown.

Where does my gift go?

To Lusega Tales' outreach in Nairobi: community meals and food packs, infant and maternal support, literacy, mentorship, women and youth empowerment, medical camps, support for persons with disabilities, and emergency relief. It also helps keep these stories being filmed and shared.

Is it safe to give through this page?

Your money goes straight from your M-Pesa to our Till number. Nothing is collected on this website. Before you confirm, M-Pesa shows you the name the till is registered to, so you can check it. If anything looks unexpected, stop and contact us on 0748 124 597.

What amount should I give?

Whatever is in your heart. Suggested gifts start at KSh 1,000 (about $8), with KSh 5,000 and KSh 10,000 for those who wish to give more. You can also type any amount you choose.

I live outside Kenya. How can I give?

Send by MoneyGram, Western Union or a similar money-transfer service to Titus Lichambi Lusega, phone 0748 124 597, Nairobi, Kenya. The exact details, with copy buttons, are in the "Giving from outside Kenya" section on this page. Afterwards, please WhatsApp us the transfer reference number and amount.

Can I give something other than money?

Yes. You can donate items, volunteer or partner with Lusega Tales. Everything is explained on the Lusega Tales Get Involved page, linked below.

Can I ask a question before I give?

Of course. Call or WhatsApp 0748 124 597, or write to info@lusegatales.com. We would rather you give with full confidence.
