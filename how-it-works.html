Our promise to your customers

We Will Never Nag Your Customer For Money

Some systems keep throwing payment prompts at a customer's phone until they get annoyed and block the business, and that is the quickest way to lose a customer for good, so ours works on one rule, your customer is always in control of when and how they pay

  • A prompt only appears when they ask for it. The M-Pesa request lands on their phone once, only after they tap the pay button
  • They can pay their own way instead. Your Till or Paybill shows in the chat with the exact amount and reference, and we match it automatically
  • Not ready? They tap "I will pay later". Their slot is held for the time you choose, then released politely
  • No money today? You decide whether a smaller deposit or part payments are allowed for each service
  • Reminders are few and polite. A couple at most, never at night, and one word, STOP, ends them for good

You still get paid on autopilot, and your customer feels respected instead of chased, which is how customers who come back and tell their friends are made
