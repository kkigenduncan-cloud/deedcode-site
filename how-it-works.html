The Process

From problem to product, step by step.

Whether you're buying a ready-made tool or building something custom, every project at DeedCode moves through the same three stages. No guesswork about what happens next.

Three stages. Every time.

1

Understand

We start by listening — to your problem, your budget, and what "done" actually looks like for you. Nothing gets built on assumptions.

Usually one honest conversation, not a 10-page intake form.

2

Build

We design and build exactly what was agreed on — clean, functional, and free of anything you didn't ask for. You'll hear from us as it takes shape, not just at the end.

Regular check-ins, so nothing arrives as a surprise.

3

Support

Launch isn't the finish line. We stay close after delivery, because software that breaks two weeks later helps no one.

A real person to call, not a support ticket into the void.

Your part is simple.

Tell us what's wrong, or what you need. Answer a few honest questions when we ask them. That's it — you don't need technical knowledge, a spec document, or the "right" words. We'll translate the rest.

How long it takes, and what it costs.

Every project is different, so we won't pretend to give you a number before we understand the problem. What we will promise: you'll know the timeline and the full cost before any work begins — never partway through, never after.

So you know exactly what to expect.

✓

No jargon-filled contracts — everything explained in plain language first.

✓

No scope creep without a conversation — if something changes, you'll know before we build it.

✓

No disappearing after delivery — support is part of the deal, not an upsell.

However you start, the process stays the same.

Honest from the first conversation to the last line of support.
