Questions

Things people ask before they start

Does it copy the poster I upload?

No, it learns from the colours, mood and layout, and builds your own design from our library, so you never copy someone else’s artwork. Please only use references you are free to use as inspiration.

Can I type exact colours?

Yes, you can type any hex code, pick colours, choose a luxury palette, describe the colours in words, or tap the reference to pick colours straight from it.

How much is the clean version?

One simple price for any poster, in your own currency, shown beside the download button.

Will it print well?

The A4 and A3 sizes are made for printing and stay sharp.

Will it copy my reference exactly?

That is the next step. The studio reads the colours and mood of your reference and gives you a matching layout to edit. A closer, element-by-element rebuild is being added, and it will appear here when ready.
