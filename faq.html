Will it work for my kind of business?

If your customers message you, book you or pay you, yes, and we already shape it for private clinics, property managers, schools and colleges, SACCOs and chamas, law, accounting and consulting firms, gyms, spas and salons, car hire, tours and driving schools, and wholesalers and distributors.

Do I need to be good with technology?

Not at all, we do the building, the connecting and the testing, then we train you and your team until you feel comfortable, and if you can use WhatsApp you can run this.

Will it sound stiff and cold to my customers?

No, we set the tone together with you so it sounds like your business, warm and plain, in English or Kiswahili, and a customer can type "agent" at any moment to reach a real person.

Do I need a Till or Paybill first?

You need one to receive money and we work with both, and if you do not have one yet, or you do not have Safaricom Daraja access, we help you get it set up.

What if my customer does not have the money right now?

Nothing bad happens, they tap "I will pay later" and the slot is held for the time you set, or you let them pay a smaller deposit or in parts, and no prompt keeps buzzing their phone.

Can my staff still reply on WhatsApp themselves?

Yes, your team can step into any chat at any moment, so the assistant handles the routine and your people handle the conversations that matter.

Can my customers order products on WhatsApp?

Yes, with the Orders and Catalogue add-on they see your products and prices in the chat, pick what they want, pay by M-Pesa, and the order lands in one list for your team.

Can I send offers to my customers?

Yes, but only to customers who have agreed to hear from you, using messages approved by Meta, and one word, STOP, ends them for good. Meta charges a small fee for messages we send first, which we list in writing before you start.

Does DeedCode ever hold our money?

No, every payment settles directly into your own Till or Paybill and we never touch it.

What will it cost besides your fees?

Safaricom charges its normal Till or Paybill fee on each payment, chats your customer starts are free for 24 hours, and reminders we send first cost roughly KES 1 to 3 each, paid straight to Meta, and we list every cost in writing before you start.

What if it stops working?

We watch it and alert you on WhatsApp if anything fails, and messages that arrive in the meantime are answered as soon as it is back.
